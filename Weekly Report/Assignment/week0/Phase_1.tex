\section*{Phase 1: Data Science Project Proposals}

\subsection*{Project 1: Housing Amenities Impact on Apartment Listing Prices in the Southern USA}

\subsubsection*{Business Issue and Overview}

Real estate investors and property managers in the Southern USA lack quantitative evidence on which amenities drive rental pricing for apartments. Understanding amenity importance enables strategic property improvements and competitive pricing strategies. This project analyzes apartment listings and determines which amenities (parking, laundry, pet policies etc.) most significantly influence rental prices in %major 
Southern apartment markets: Austin TX, Dallas TX, Miami FL, Atlanta GA, Nashville TN, and Charlotte NC. %Geographic focus is essential: amenity value in Austin apartment complexes differs significantly from Miami or Atlanta, requiring region-specific analysis of apartment rental markets.

\subsubsection*{Business Questions}

\begin{itemize} [itemsep = -9pt]
    \item Which amenities have strongest impact on rental pricing?
    \item How does amenity importance vary across geographic regions?
    \item Which amenity upgrades yield the highest price premium relative to cost?
    \item Do certain amenity bundles (e.g., parking \& laundry) have compounding effects on price?
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Data Needs}

Major entities: Apartment rental listings from Southern markets (Austin, Dallas, Miami, Atlanta, Nashville, Charlotte) including %rental 
price, location, bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, amenities (parking, laundry, pet policies, etc), listing metadata. %Requires at least two datasets for robust analysis: primary sources include Zillow apartment listings and Apartments.com data combined, supplemented by regional property management MLS databases.

\subsubsection*{Reason}

Addresses urgent business problem in high-growth Southern real estate markets. Understanding which amenities justify investment is directly actionable for property managers operating in Southern markets. Regional focus ensures recommendations are relevant to Southern investors rather than generic national insights. %Deepens familiarity with feature importance techniques and regression modeling while producing region-specific competitive insights.

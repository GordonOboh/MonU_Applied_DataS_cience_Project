When writing or editing any .tex file, replace all em dashes (—) with contextually appropriate punctuation before finishing.

**Why:** User preference for cleaner LaTeX source and consistent punctuation style.

**How to apply:**
- Em dash introducing a consequence or explanation → colon (e.g., "result: details")
- Em dash as a parenthetical aside → parentheses (e.g., "word (aside) word")
- Em dash as a pause or list connector → comma
- After replacing, verify readability and tone are preserved
- Do not change any other content
